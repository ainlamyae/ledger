Your personal life dashboard

Health, finances, time tracking, travel, applications, and contacts — all in one dashboard, backed by your own Google Sheet with no third-party servers.

At a Glance & Private by Design

A live clock with a second time zone, today's date in three calendars, and the local weather — right above your numbers. Your data lives in your own private Google Sheet — never on a third-party server. Privacy mode masks all amounts on screen at a keystroke.

Health Tracker

Track Body Weight, Caloric Intake, Protein Intake, Physical Activity, and Rest & Recovery in a searchable Health Log. Charts show your last 14 days versus daily targets.

Weight Forecast

A Weight Forecast chart projects your goal arrival date, with a Calibrate button that fits the formula to your own logged history instead of generic constants — a personalized model calibrated from your own history of weight, calorie, activity, and sleep data.

AI Calorie & Protein Estimator

Type what you ate in plain language and let AI do the math — ingredients are extracted automatically, and both calories and protein are grounded against the USDA nutrition database for an accurate estimate.

AI Health Insight

Get a plain-language read on your recent habits: a 7/14/30-day snapshot compared against your own targets and trend, reviewed by AI in a closable report — nothing is sent until you ask, and nothing is saved.

Journal

Add, edit, search, filter, sort, and bulk-edit your Transaction History, with autocomplete for payees and categories and an undo on every delete. Flip on Advanced Filters for a date range plus AND/OR field rules, and export exactly what's filtered to CSV.

Portfolio

Track every account's balance in one Account Summary, see your allocation by type and institution in a nested donut chart, and get an instant reconciliation check against your transaction history.

Analytics Dashboard

See your net worth, monthly cash flow, and portfolio allocation at a glance — with Revenue vs. Expenditure and Cumulative Net Worth trends.

Expenditure Insights

Compare this month's expenditure to quarterly and yearly averages, category by category — with type-level breakdowns and a Spending Patterns panel.

Time Tracker

Log daily work hours, detect missed entries, and visualise arrival, departure, and hours-worked patterns with Work Analytics histogram charts.

Travel & Applications

Log every trip and see a Time Spent by Country breakdown plus a world map of visited countries. Track immigration/visa applications — grouped into Ongoing and Closed — from submission through every status update to final decision.

Contacts

Keep a searchable Contact List with names, phones, emails, addresses, birthdays, and social links. Bulk-select rows to export CSVs for Google/Phone or Outlook, or merge duplicate entries into one.
