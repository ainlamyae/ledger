## Health — Chart

Every chart and tile below reads the **`Physique`** tab — one row per day — via `physiqueAsHealthEntries()` (`health-physique.js`), which expands each day back into the per-event shape `health-math.js`/`health-chart.js` consume. It is the only tab any of them read.

- **Tune**, opening the Tune, sits in this panel's own heading — the panel whose charts and tiles read every one of its settings.

- All charts share one height and one plot-area width, so their date labels line up down the page.
- **One row of range buttons — 1w, 1m (default), 3m, 6m, 1y, 5y, All — above the State Trend & Forecast heading, is the panel's window**: Market's button row (`renderRangeButtons`), each a fixed length ending today; All starts at the first logged Physique day. The old From/To inputs are commented out in `index.html` and `health-chart.js`. It covers State Trend & Forecast, Body Mass, Calorie Balance, Caloric Intake, Protein Intake, Dietary Fiber Intake, Fat Intake, Carbohydrate Intake, Physical Activity, Sleep, Protein Source Rotation and Activity Rotation all plot it, and all redraw together on a change. Default is the last 4 weeks (`HEALTH_METRICS_DAYS`). Protein Source Rotation used to carry a second pair of its own, so the panel showed two windows at once.
  - **State Trend & Forecast follows it on both ends, forecast included** — nothing plots outside the window, the same as every other chart here never draws a "tomorrow" bar. With the default To (today), that means the projected segment is invisible until To is pushed into the future; there's nothing chart-specific about that, it's the same rule the rest of the panel already lives by.
  - `healthDateRange()` reads the two inputs straight from the DOM, falling back to the default when either is blank — so it can't matter whether a chart renders before or after the control is wired.
  - **Every chart reads the exact same date list** (`healthCalorieChartDates`, clipped forward to the earliest **calorie** entry ever logged) rather than each clipping to its own metric's own earliest log — a Sleep chart clipped to its own first entry could pick a different-length list than Body Mass, and two charts running the identical tick-selection algorithm over two different-length lists land on different calendar dates even though both look "evenly spaced". One shared list is what lets every chart's gridlines agree.
  - **8 evenly spaced vertical gridlines** (`HEALTH_X_GRIDLINES`, `wellnessTickIndices`, `charts-base.js`) on every dated chart (all but Protein Source Rotation and Activity Rotation): the first on the window's first date at the plot's left edge and the last on its last date at the right edge at every range (no half-day margin, so a bar on either end is half shown), the rest at index `round(i × (n − 1) / 7)`; a window of 8 days or fewer gets one per day. Every one of these charts shares one date list (`healthCalorieChartDates`: from the first calorie log or body-mass reading in range), so their gridlines land on the same dates.
  - **One left y-axis width on every chart** (`TREND_Y_AXIS_WIDTH`, `charts-base.js`: 120px), wide enough for an 18-character Rotation name such as "Protein Supplement"; longer names end in `…` (`trendYAxisCategoryTick`). Right axes stay 84px. Equal widths keep every plot area, and so every gridline, lined up.
  - **Every legend entry is a switch** (`renderToggleLegend`, `charts-base.js`): click to hide its series (a line, cap or zone) or its bars/dots of that colour (`maskHiddenClasses`), click again to bring it back; hidden entries stay dimmed and struck through, and stay hidden across range changes until the page reloads.
  - An inverted range yields no days, which every caller already reads as "nothing to draw".
- **State Trend & Forecast** — body mass history, smoothed trend, and a projection toward the healthy body mass; optional BMI twin axis. **Currently hidden** (canvas commented out in `index.html`); its Calorie-Implied Trajectory, glycogen swing zone and Muscle Loss band have been moved to the **Body Mass** chart below. The progress meters and plateau alert remain active in the DOM and continue to populate.
  - **Its own date list is filtered from the sparse points themselves (real body-mass reading days, real weekly forecast points), not built from `healthWindowDates()`** — that helper returns every calendar day in the window, which is what the bar charts below want (an unlogged day still draws an empty column) but is wrong here: this chart's series sit on a linear day-offset axis with `spanGaps: false`, so a run of dateless "empty" days inserted next to the sparse weekly forecast points would leave `spanGaps` nothing to bridge between them and break the dashed projection into invisible fragments.
  - **"Arrival" is judged past the healthy body mass by the glycogen swing**, in the direction travel is already headed (e.g. a 70 kg healthy mass on a cut counts as reached once the trend passes 68.1 kg at a 1.9 kg swing) — so a bad-water-day reading can't land on the wrong side of the real healthy mass. This governs the projection's day count, ETA and "Desired mass reached!" status; the progress bar's raw kg-to-go and the drawn healthy-mass line still read the exact healthy mass.
  - Progress meters, in their own full-width card under the Status glance cards (not in this panel): distance covered and time elapsed, side by side, read as "lost" (or "gained" on a bulk) / "left" in kg and "elapsed" / "ahead" in days — each "left" and "ahead" figure also names its end point (healthy body mass — shown with its `± swing` alongside it when the swing is known — or the projected arrival date). Both tracks carry marks at every 10% and fill in five colour bands — red, orange, amber, yellow-green, green — so each fifth reached shows as a visible step toward the goal; unreached bands stay grey, and a wrong-direction bar is still solid red.
  - Plateau alert when the smoothed trend has held flat.
  - The status line under the chart speaks only when there is **no** forecast to draw — healthy mass reached, no net change, trending away, or levelling off short of the healthy mass. A forecast that renders says nothing, since the meters and the curve already do.
- **Body Mass** — one dot per reading (same dot style as Physical Activity's own Actual Burn series — `pointRadius`/`pointBorderColor`/`pointBorderWidth`), joined by a thin gray line that spans days with no reading, scored by direction of travel (the first reading after a gap of over 14 days is unscored, and the 7-Day Trend restarts there); its dates start at the first calorie log or body-mass reading in range, whichever is earlier; left axis in kg, right axis in BMI (kg/m²), both with matched tick density.
  - **m_des (Healthy Body Mass)** — Tune's healthy body mass (`BODY_MASS_HEALTHY_KG`) marked on every day with the section's desired-value cap (`desiredCapDataset`) in cyan, so it reads apart from the 7-Day Trend, the y axis widened to keep it in view, and stated once in the hover as `m_des (Healthy Body Mass): <kg> kg`.
  - **Legend** under the chart (`renderCategoryLegend`, as Protein Source Rotation): Toward healthy, Away from healthy, Unscored, 7-Day Trend, Healthy Body Mass, Calorie Trend, Glycogen + Water Swing, Muscle Loss — each only when drawn.
  - **A red day regrades to grey when the trend isn't actually going the wrong way** — if that reading rose (or fell, for a bulk) against the healthy mass but the smoothed curve's own trailing-7-day rate still points the right direction, the single reading reads as noise rather than a miss.
  - **Carries three series that belong to State Trend & Forecast** (currently hidden), all computed off the full history and read back for just the windowed dates:
    - **Calorie-Implied Trajectory** (solid indigo `--chart-indigo`, so it reads apart from the thin gray line joining the dots) — walks `dailyEnergyBalanceKcal` day by day from the first reading of the latest run (a gap over 14 days between readings starts a new run, so a lone old reading can't anchor it), replayed as a running body-mass total. The gap between it and the smoothed trend the tooltip quotes is what logged calories alone can't explain — water, glycogen, or a logging gap. Needs a full profile (height/age/sex); omitted without one.
    - **Glycogen/water swing zone** (translucent amber) — anchored to the Calorie-Implied Trajectory: the top edge sits exactly on the indigo line, and the band runs from there down by the full `glycogenSwingKg` figure (`ΔM_gly`). Anchoring to the trajectory rather than the smoothed trend keeps the zone's meaning honest: the trajectory is where the scale *should* read from calories alone, and the amber band is the glycogen noise on top of that.
    - **Muscle Loss band** (translucent red) fills below the amber zone, marking the region where actual mass would imply lean-tissue loss rather than fat alone.
  - The hover shows **`Changed Mass`** in grams (not kg) and a **`Smoothed Change`** slope in g/day (`computeBodyMassTrendSlopeGramsPerDay`); **BMI** is shown with a `kg/m²` unit suffix.
  - **`Calorie Trend` / `Calorie Trend Slope`**, right above `7-Day Trend` in the `afterBody` block — the indigo Calorie-Implied Trajectory line's own value and that day's own balance-implied change, since the overlay lines are otherwise filtered out of the hover entirely (`isStateTrendOverlay`) and never showed their own numbers anywhere. The slope is read from a per-date balance map captured while the trajectory is built, not by diffing two plotted points — `calorieTrendMap.get(d)` is the trajectory as of the *start* of `d`, so a point-to-point diff actually reads the prior day's balance. Captured directly, it's exactly the Status card's own Δm arithmetic, so the two agree for today.
- **Calorie Balance** — intake minus BMR minus activity, scored against the *desired* deficit; grams-of-fat twin axis. The hover lists TEI, all five stored BMR figures (`BMR_mif`, `BMR_kat`, `BMR_cal`, `BMR_adp`, `BMR_avg`), AEE, TEF and SD on every day (SD shows 0 rather than disappearing), then D (Desired …) and the 7-Day Average. Every line reads `label (definition): number unit`, and only negatives carry a sign.
  - The hover spells out the subtraction and **signs it**: `Actual Intake` as the figure it starts from, then `Maintenance` and `Activity` as negatives, so the column reads top-down as the arithmetic behind the bar. Activity printed as a positive read as something added to the day, which is the opposite of what burning it does. The intake row carries the same name Caloric Intake and Protein Intake use in their own hovers — one day's eating shouldn't be called three different things across one panel.
  - **Sleep Deprivation Effect** — a line in the hover on every day (0 when the night met the desired sleep or none was logged), for what a short night cost the day (`dailyEnergyBalanceKcal`, `health-math.js`). Added, not subtracted: on a deficit day it's how much less of it became fat loss; on a surplus day it's how much *more* was gained, since short sleep drives hunger and cuts NEAT rather than sitting out surplus days — the adjustment always pushes the balance toward the positive side, whichever side of zero the day started on. Folded straight into the bar/`Actual Deficit`/`Actual Surplus` figure itself, so this chart and State Trend & Forecast's gray Calorie-Implied Trajectory (which walks the same day-by-day arithmetic) can't disagree about what a night cost.
  - **Legend** under the chart (`renderCategoryLegend`): Desired met, Week on desired pace, Missed desired, 7-Day Average, and the desired dash, now cyan so it reads apart from the 7-Day Average; with no desired figure, Toward desired / Away from desired.
- **Caloric Intake** — per-day bars with a per-day desired intake drawn as a cap on each bar, not one shared line. The y axis starts at 0 kcal, so a bar's height is the day's intake; its top fits what was logged, raised to reach any desired or BMR cap above it.
  - **On a cut (Desired Max), a bar past the desired intake gradients rather than jumping straight to red** — green at the desired intake itself, sliding to red at that day's own Basal Metabolic Rate (below), since eating up to BMR alone already erases the deficit before activity or TEF even factor in: the real ceiling the desired intake sits a step under. Solid red at or past BMR itself. A bulk's Desired Min has no such ceiling to gradient toward, and neither does a cut day whose BMR can't be priced yet (no profile, or no body mass logged) — both keep the flat near (grey, within the near-margin)/missed (red) split instead.
  - **A solid-red bar (at/past BMR, or the flat-split "missed") regrades to grey when the 7-day average is still on the desired intake's right side** — the same "one bad day, good week" read Body Mass gets, scored against the smoothed average instead of its rate.
  - **Five BMR caps — `BMR_mif` (blue), `BMR_kat` (orange), `BMR_cal` (indigo), `BMR_adp` (purple), `BMR_avg` (amber) — read from each day's stored Derived cell** (Physique's Derived column), at that day's own carried-forward body mass and age; previously computed here (`carryForwardBodyMassByDate`, `ageFromBirthDate(birthDateStr, asOf)`) through `bmrKcal` — not a flat figure at today's, since a multi-week window can span a birthday and body mass is the whole point of the chart beside it. Same `desiredCapDataset` cap idiom as the Desired line above and Protein/Fiber Intake's own min/max, at this chart's own `capHalf`, not a separate line style. Restated in the hover's `afterBody` alongside Desired and the 7-Day Average. `ageFromBirthDate`'s optional `asOf` parameter (default today) is what makes this per-day read possible — every other caller still asks "how old are they now."
  - **Legend** under the chart (`renderCategoryLegend`): Desired met, Near desired, Missed desired, 7-Day Average, Desired Max (or Min) — the cap, cyan as on Body Mass and Calorie Balance — and each BMR drawn, by its `BMR_DEFINITIONS` name (Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, Katch-McArdle BMR, Calibrated BMR, Adapted BMR by t, Average BMR).
  - All four are shown regardless of which basis `BMR_BASIS` has `D`/`Δm` running on, and the hover lists them as `BMR_mif (Mifflin-St Jeor BMR): 1693 kcal` and so on. `BMR_adp` is that day's BMR × its own `(1 − λt)`; `BMR_cal` is the rolling [calibration](health-tune.md#health--bmr-calibration-in-tune) offset over the n_p × L_p days before it, absent in the first n_p × L_p days of data.
- **Protein Intake** — bars against a shaded desired band; over the top end is a ceiling, not extra credit. Dual y-axes: grams on the right, the equivalent kcal (g × 4) on the left.
  - **Legend** under the chart (`renderCategoryLegend`): Within desired, Over desired, Under desired, 7-Day Average, Desired Protein (the caps, cyan as on the other charts) and Desired Protein band.
- **Dietary Fiber Intake** — the same shaded-band treatment as Protein Intake (`getFiberDesiredBandG`, shared caps, same three-way red/green/dark-green split), rather than the flat single-desired line it used before the Tune gained a fiber band. Falls back to one flat cap, same as Protein's zero-width case, until that band is saved. Dual y-axes: grams on the right, kcal (g × 2) on the left.
  - **Legend** under the chart: Within desired, Over desired, Under desired, 7-Day Average, Desired Dietary Fiber (the caps, now cyan) and its band.
- **Fat Intake** — same shaded-band fill as Protein/Fiber (green, behind the bars) and the same floor/band/ceiling split (`getFatDesiredBandG`/`withinFatBand`), but past the ceiling is a lighter green rather than Fiber's darker one — still inside the AMDR's own upper bound, not a genuine miss. The band edges themselves draw as one continuous dashed line across the whole window (`desiredMarkColor()`, the same near-black/near-white every desired cap on the panel uses) rather than Fiber's per-day caps — Fiber's floor and ceiling come off two different bases, so per-day caps read as two independent desired figures; Fat's two ends are both a share of the same E_in, so a flat dashed line at each edge already says "this held all window". Falls back to a flat 65g (the USDA %DV reference for a 2,000 kcal diet), same shape as Fiber's own flat fallback, until the Tune's fat band is saved. Dual y-axes: grams on the right, kcal (g × 9) on the left.
  - **Legend** under the chart: Within desired, Over desired (light green), Under desired, 7-Day Average, Desired Fat (the dashed lines, now cyan) and its band.
- **Carbohydrate Intake** — Fat Intake's chart again, one column over: same shaded band, same dashed-line-not-per-day-caps edges (`getCarbDesiredBandG`/`withinCarbBand`), since carb's two ends are also both a share of E_in — but the opposite severity read. Too little carbohydrate isn't a deficiency the way too little fiber or protein is, so under the floor is unscored grey rather than a red miss; it's *over* the ceiling that's flagged red — the end actually linked to the bloating/water-retention that comes with the glycogen it gets stored as. Falls back to a flat 275g (the USDA %DV reference for total carbohydrate on a 2,000 kcal diet) until the Tune's carb band is saved. Read from the Physique day's own `Carbohydrate` column (`carbG`), the same per-day pipeline Fiber/Fat already ride. Dual y-axes: grams on the right, kcal (g × 4) on the left.
  - **Legend** under the chart: Within desired, Over desired (red), Under desired (gray), 7-Day Average, Desired Carbohydrate (the dashed lines, now cyan) and its band.
- **Physical Activity** — stacked minutes per activity type, plus a calories-burned dot series. The type is derived from the day's `Workout` lines by the same `describeExerciseNames()` Log a Workout uses ("Strength Training", "NEAT", "Cardio", "NEAT + Cardio"); a workout line whose exercise isn't on the Activities tab is dropped rather than grouped under an "Other" segment. Sits directly above Sleep, the two now adjacent in the donut-grid order (they used to bookend it, Activity first and Sleep last).
  - **Legend** under the chart: one swatch per activity in its bar colour, then the burn dots (Desired met, Near desired, Missed desired), 7-Day Average Burn and Desired Burn (the cap, now cyan).
- **Sleep** — floating bars spanning bed→wake on a clock-time axis, coloured by adherence, on a **right-side** clock-time axis now that the left one carries the Deprivation Effect dot below.
  - **Deprivation Effect dot** — one point per night, plotted on the left axis against `Desired Deficit × (1 − Sleep Efficiency Factor)` (the plan's own flat desired deficit, `desiredBalanceKcal`, discounted for that one night — not the day's actual eaten-vs-burned balance, which is Calorie Balance's job instead). Zero — green — at a full night against the `SLEEP_DESIRED_HOURS` setting; red and rising the further that night fell short, the colour scaled to the kcal value itself (`sleepDeprivationDotColor`, full red at 60 kcal/day) rather than to hours short, so the colour can't tell a different story than the number beside it. The tooltip adds its own line, `Deprivation Effect: N kcal`, alongside the night's own Bed/Wake/Duration.
  - **Sleep Efficiency Factor** (η) is `1 − (γ/100) × max(0, SLEEP_DESIRED_HOURS − sleepHours)` — see the Tune's `s`/`γ`/`δ` fields below, which set the plan-level assumption this dot's desired deficit is itself built from.
  - **Legend** under the chart: Desired sleep met, Short of desired, Half of desired or less (the bar gradient), 7-Day Average Bed & Wake (one colour, one entry) and the Sleep Deprivation Effect dot.
- **Protein Source Rotation** and **Activity Rotation** last — see their sections below.
- All nine of the scored charts carry a **solid 7-day trend line**, so a stretch that quietly drifted past its desired figure is visible next to the per-day mark.
  - In the panel's own near-black/near-white neutral (`desiredMarkColor()`), the same colour every desired cap on the panel uses — deliberately not a scored green/red/grey, and no longer the dashed violet segments earlier versions of this line used.
  - **A continuous exponential moving average, not a fixed weekly bucket** (`emaSeries`) — today's own reading blends straight into yesterday's already-smoothed value, so there's no seam where a calendar week resets. `monotone` cubic interpolation keeps the curve from overshooting past a local high or low into a bump the data never had.
  - **Today isn't held out.** A day logged just this morning updates the line the moment it's entered, the same as any other day.
  - Built from days that were actually **logged**; an unlogged day carries the last smoothed value forward rather than breaking the line.
  - Sleep carries two, smoothed bedtime and smoothed wake time. Physical Activity smooths the *calories burned*, not the minutes, since that's the axis Desired Burn lives on.
  - **Body Mass alone also derives a rate** (`emaSlopePerSpan`) — the smoothed curve's own change over the trailing 7 days, comparable to a per-week slope but read continuously rather than re-fit each week. Quoted in its tooltip as `kg/week`.
  - Every one of these charts adds its own figure to the tooltip as well.

## Health — Protein Source Rotation

- Last block of the **Chart** panel — every Health chart sits in that one panel, so they collapse together and share its one range window.
- The donut's two rings are fixed spans anchored to the window's **To** date (4 weeks and 1 week), not fractions of the window — so they keep meaning the same thing whatever range is picked.
- One horizontal bar per Nutrition **Group** that has at least one ingredient carrying a Protein % (`groupProteinRotationRows`).
- Bar is actual protein eaten in range from that group's tracked ingredients, stacked by ingredient (one shade of the group's hue each); a red tick marks its live desired share — the sum of its members' Protein % shares. Protein % is typed freely, so the tracked ingredients' values are scaled to add up to 100% (10, 20, 30 become 16.7%, 33.3%, 50%), and those shares are taken of the **top** of the desired protein band (`getProteinDesiredBandG().max`). The donut's reference ring and Insight's Protein mode show the same scaled shares.
- Hovering a bar segment names that ingredient and its grams; hovering a slice lists the ingredients the group is summed from.
- One hue per group. Groups ordered by remaining gap, most-left-to-eat first; `Unclassified` last.
- A legend below shows one swatch per group, and a second line one swatch per ingredient in its stack shade.
- Beside the bars, a donut splits the same groups by share: reference Protein %, then 4 weeks, then last week.
- The per-ingredient figures (`computeProteinRotationRows`) are unchanged — Insight's Protein mode still reads them.

## Health — Activity Rotation

- Same shape as Protein Source Rotation directly above it — one horizontal bar chart and one rotational donut — and shares its panel and range window.
- One bar per Activity **Group** (Leg Day, Push Day, NEAT, Cardio, …) carrying a Weekly Desired — a new column on the `Activity` sheet tab, shared by every exercise row in that Group.
- A "session" is any day whose logged Workout notes include at least one exercise from that Group — a mixed day (a lift plus a walk) counts as a session for every Group it touches, not just the dominant one.
- Bar is sessions actually logged in range; a red tick marks the Weekly Desired scaled to the window's length.
- **Coloured by Category** (the sheet's Strength/Cardio/NEAT column) — one hue per category, lightness stepped within it, same legend/coloring convention as Protein Source Rotation.
- Groups ordered by remaining gap (desired minus actual), as in Protein Source Rotation: furthest below the desired sessions first, down to those that meet or pass it; `Unclassified` last.
- Beside the bars, a three-ring donut: outer ring each Group's own Weekly Desired (the reference split), middle ring the last 4 weeks, inner ring the last week.
- **A copy of this card also sits under Workouts's own tiles** (`/health/activity/workouts/`) — see [Health — Activity & Workouts](health-activity.md#health--activity-plan).
