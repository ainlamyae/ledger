    Amount needs either a mass in grams somewhere in it (e.g. "100g" or "1 scoop (32g)") or, for a food that's naturally counted in whole pieces, a leading count (e.g. "1 rice cake", "2 eggs") — either way Calculate can scale it to whatever quantity you actually logged.
    
            
    Protein % sets what share of your protein target should come from this ingredient (e.g. 10 for "10% of my protein target") — since that target already updates live with your body mass/activity, this ingredient's actual gram target moves with it automatically.
